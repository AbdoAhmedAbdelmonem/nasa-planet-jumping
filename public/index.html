8-Dimensional Environmental Feature Matrix

How Deimos pairs off-world craters with terrestrial basins

Every surface coordinate on the Moon and Mars is evaluated across 8 fundamental planetary features and cross-matched against our database of 268,252 high-resolution terrestrial sites via spatial L2 KNN distance ranking.

01. SURFACE GRAVITY

Gravitational Acceleration

Compares extreme gravitational acceleration—Lunar 1.62 m/s² and Martian 3.72 m/s² against Earth's 9.81 m/s² baseline, governing traction, sinkage, and regolith bearing strength.

Dynamic Range 1.62 – 9.81 m/s²
02. ATMOSPHERIC PRESSURE

Barometric Density

Tracks atmospheric columns from near-vacuum (0 kPa on the Moon) and thin carbon dioxide (0.6–0.8 kPa on Mars) to Earth's high-altitude and sea-level barometric envelopes.

Pressure Span 0.0 – 101.3 kPa
03. DAY SURFACE TEMP

Solar Radiation Peaks

Models diurnal midday thermal highs under unshielded solar flux, tracking lunar peaks up to +120°C and Martian equator highs up to +20°C against Earth hyper-arid deserts.

Midday Window -45°C to +120°C
04. NIGHT SURFACE TEMP

Cryogenic Night Plunge

Calculates nocturnal radiative cooling down to cryogenic troughs below -170°C on the Moon and -125°C on Mars against terrestrial Antarctic permafrost regimes.

Nocturnal Limit -170°C to -10°C
05. SURFACE ELEVATION

Altimetry & Relief

Cross-queries LOLA and MOLA orbital laser altimetry models against Earth terrain baselines, matching deep impact basins, rift canyons, and massive volcanic calderas.

Elevation Span -8,200 m to +21,287 m
06. TERRAIN SLOPE

Incline Gradients

Quantifies local slope angles, crater rim scarps, and dune escarpments to evaluate rover slip hazard, wheel-sinkage bearing capacity, and traverse safety.

Incline Range 0.0° to 45.0° Gradients
07. SURFACE ROUGHNESS

Obstacle & RMS Hazards

Measures root-mean-square roughness and boulder size-frequency distributions across grid chunks to pinpoint smooth landing corridors versus rugged rock fields.

Roughness RMS 0.1 m to 85.0 m
08. SURFACE ALBEDO

Optical Photometrics

Matches surface reflectance signatures—distinguishing dark basaltic lunar mare sheets and rusty Martian dust from bright anorthositic highlands and volcanic sands.

Albedo Index 0.05 to 0.45 Reflectance
